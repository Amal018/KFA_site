About Kalalaya

A Place Where Creativity
Has No Limits

Established in 2002, Kalalaya Fine Arts is a leading drawing and painting institute in Coimbatore dedicated to nurturing creativity and artistic expression.

With more than two decades of experience in art education, we provide structured training for children, teenagers and adults in a supportive environment.

Classes Near You

Drawing, Painting & Art Classes in Coimbatore

Looking for a drawing class, painting class or art class near you? Kalalaya Fine Arts has taught in Coimbatore since 2002, from our studio on 511/1, Rabindranath Tagore Road, Maniyakarampalayam. Families join us from Maniyakarampalayam, Ganapathy, Sanganur, Rathinapuri, Gandhipuram, Saravanampatti, Peelamedu, Thudiyalur, Kavundampalayam and Vilankurichi.

  • Drawing classesPencil, shading, portraits & sketching

Quick answers

Where is Kalalaya Fine Arts located?

We are at 511/1, Rabindranath Tagore Road, Maniyakarampalayam, Coimbatore 641006, Tamil Nadu — open Monday – Saturday, 9:00 AM – 7:00 PM.

Which ages do you teach?

Children from age 5, school students up to 18, and adults. Each age group follows its own structured syllabus.

Do you have weekend batches?

Yes. Students aged 8–18 can attend Saturday evening (5–6:30 pm) or Sunday morning (11 am–12:30 pm, or the special batch from 10:30 am). Kids batches run on weekday evenings.

How do I enrol?

Tap “Reserve a Seat” to send your details on WhatsApp, call +91 98424 34219, or visit the studio.
